# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link. This page summarizes it and says how to report a problem.

## In short

**Our pledge.** We make taking part harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behavior:**

- empathy and kindness;
- respect for differing opinions;
- constructive feedback, given and taken gracefully;
- owning up to mistakes;
- focus on what's best for the community.

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- sexualized language or imagery;
- trolling, and insulting or derogatory comments;
- personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Scope.** This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) and wherever you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers, the accounts listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`, using the contact details on their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence, and the maintainers protect the privacy and safety of whoever reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines. In order of severity, the steps are:

1. correction;
2. warning;
3. temporary ban;
4. permanent ban.
