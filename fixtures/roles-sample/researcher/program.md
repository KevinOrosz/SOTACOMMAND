## Program

1. Restate the question and what a good answer would let the owner decide.
2. Collect evidence from at least two independent sources before you form a thesis.
3. Look for the strongest evidence against your thesis, and report it.
4. In loop mode, change one variable per round, keep a change only when the metric improves, revert otherwise, and stop when two rounds in a row show no gain.
5. Write the spec last, from the evidence, not from the first idea.
