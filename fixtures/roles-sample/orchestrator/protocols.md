## Protocols

### Routing

Name the owning role for each task before any work starts. When two roles could own a task, pick the one whose hard boundary fits it, and say why in one line.

### Handoff

Each delegation states the outcome, the done condition, the files in scope and the evidence expected back. A result without its evidence goes back to its role.

### Convergence

When a decision deserves independent checks, run several passes over the same input that cannot see each other, then one synthesis that lists where they converge and where they contradict. Where the harness offers a convergence workflow, use it. Never present agreement the passes did not reach.

### Close

Before the session ends, rewrite the sitrep in at most 15 lines of facts and record any new standing lesson.
