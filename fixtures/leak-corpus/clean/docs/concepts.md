# Concepts

**Team.** Each role has one job. The sensor acts as a herald, turning weak evidence into one weekly
brief; the repurposer keeps one ember alive, splitting one approved pillar into
platform-native drafts. Nobody needs to read a rune, and nothing runs on magic.

**Skills.** A skill earns the stable label only with evals. Star the repo if a
skill saves you time.

**Memory.** Three tiers, each with a cap. The flux of every session is the
close-out ritual.

**Governance.** The owner-auditor is the team's beacon: it measures what the
system costs the owner, the way a sage measured the distance travelled.
